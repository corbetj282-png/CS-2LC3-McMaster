For understanding these, keep in mind that _<_ has type “ℤ → ℤ → 𝔹”, so the two one-argument predicates “_< r” and “r <_” (notation not
yet supported by CalcCheck) that result from < by supplying r as one of the two arguments both have type “ℤ → 𝔹”, and the corresponding
antitonicity respectively monotonicity properties therefore have an antecedent expressing ordering in ℤ (via the order ≤) and a consequence
expressing ordering in 𝔹 (via the order ⇒).
This type situation is the same for _≤_ as for _<_, and the corresponding properties hold also there:
