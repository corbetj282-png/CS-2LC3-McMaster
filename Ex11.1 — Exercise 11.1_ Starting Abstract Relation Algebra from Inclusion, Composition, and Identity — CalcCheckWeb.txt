[1]

This notebook is the first is a sequence on abstract relation algebra, which is concerned with reasoning about relations without descending to
the level of the related elements.
Therefore, the relationship notation _⦗_⦘_ is not available here, and it the point of view of concrete relations, that is, of
“a binary relation is a set of pairs”,
is not relevant here.
That said, concrete relations do form a model of the theory developed under the label “abstract relation algebra”. In other words, concrete
relations, implemented as sets of pairs, and their operations satisfy the axioms presented here in abstract relation algebra, and therefore all the
theorems developed in the abstract setting are valid for concrete relations as well.
