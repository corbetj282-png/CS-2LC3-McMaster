Abstract Kleene Algebra: General Properties of Iteration
[2]

Reflexive-transitive closure _* of relations satisfies many algebraic properties that it shares with iteration of regular languages, which supplies the
meaning of _* as an operator in the context of regular expressions.
Abstract algebraic treatment of “Kleene star” _* usually is set in a context where composition ⨾, identities 𝕀, inclusion ordering ⊆, and union ∪
are available. Therefore reference notebook 10.1, which is preloaded here, builds, besides on propositional logic, only on the reference
notebooks 9.2 and 9.5.
In that context, the iteration operators are declared as follows:
Declaration: _⁺ , _* : A ↔ A

→

A ↔ A

Abstract Kleene algebra nowadays usually starts from the following three axioms:
Axiom (KA.1) “Definition of *”:
R * = 𝕀 ∪ R ∪ R * ⨾ R *
Axiom (KA.2) “Left-induction for *”: R ⨾ S ⊆ S ⇒ R * ⨾ S ⊆ S
Axiom (KA.3) “Right-induction for *”: Q ⨾ R ⊆ Q ⇒ Q ⨾ R * ⊆ Q
The “at-least-one-step version” _⁺ is derived easily:
Axiom (KA.4) “Definition of ⁺”:

R ⁺ = R ⨾ R *


(Remember that ↔ has higher precedence than →, and ⨾ has higher precedence than ∪.)
