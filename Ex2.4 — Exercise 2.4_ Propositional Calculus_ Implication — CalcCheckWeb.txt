The fact that the theorems (3.57) and (3.60) each can be turned into three different two-argument
equivalences can increase the search space for rewriting considerably. In most cases, these two theorems
are used just to expand an implication, making exploration of the other equivalences superfluous.
We therefore provide also variants presented with a top-level equality “=”, which avoids the other,
unwanted, equivalences. We avoid giving them overlapping names, since that would undo the
specialisation effect and produce even more duplicated work.
