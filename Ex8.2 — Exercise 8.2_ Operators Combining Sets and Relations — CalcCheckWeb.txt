Domain and Range Restrictions
[5]

The domain and range restrictions have the same precedence as intersection _∩_ and union _∪_, to avoid ambiguities induced by inadvertent
omission of parentheses.
The domain restrictions associate to the right, and the range restrictions to the left — only this makes sense given their types.
The Unicode code points for the symbols for these operators are specified by the Z standard; browsers may unfortunately display them at
vastly different sizes.
For input,
type “\drestr” for “◁” — “domain restriction”,
type “\ndrestr” for “⩤” — “negated domain restriction”, “domain antirestriction”,
type “\rrestr” for “▷” — “range restriction”,
type “\nrrestr” for “⩥” — “negated range restriction”, “range antirestriction”.
